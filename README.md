## Overview
Mytransport is a full-featured Transport Management System (TMS) built natively on the Frappe framework. It provides transporters, fleet owners, and logistics companies with a seamless, unified workflow to track shipments, manage vehicles, and automate accounting.

By integrating directly with ERPNext's accounting engine, Mytransport eliminates double-data entry and ensures your books are always accurate and up-to-date.

## Key Features

### 📦 Lorry Receipts (LR)
Generate and track Lorry Receipts easily. Record consignor, consignee, origin, destination, and exact freight details. Track the real-time status of every shipment from dispatch to delivery and billing.

### 🚚 Trip Challans
Batch multiple Lorry Receipts into a single Trip Challan. Assign vehicles, brokers, and drivers. Define total trip hire amounts and advance payments. Automatically track the profitability of every trip in real-time.

### 💸 Expense Vouchers & Advances
Pay transport vendors or brokers directly from the system. Generate Expense Vouchers that automatically map to Frappe Journal Entries, securely logging trip advances against the `Creditors - TC` accounting ledger.

### 🧾 Transport Invoicing
Bill your customers effortlessly. Combine multiple delivered Lorry Receipts into a unified Transport Invoice. Includes automated calculation of basic freight, hamali charges, bilty charges, and dynamic outstanding balances.

### 💰 Money Receipts
Log customer payments against specific Transport Invoices. Handle partial payments and automatic status updates seamlessly integrated with your chart of accounts.

## Reports & Analytics
- **Challan Costing Report**: Compare expected revenue against trip expenses to analyze real net profit per vehicle or route.
- **LR Status & Registers**: Maintain complete visibility over unpaid, unbilled, and pending shipments.
